Privacy Policy

Stream Highlights is a private tool used only by its owner. It does not collect, store or share personal data of any other person.

When the owner signs in with TikTok, an access token for the owner's own account is stored locally on the owner's computer. It is used only to upload video drafts to that account and is never sent to third parties. The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app settings.

No analytics, tracking or advertising is used.
